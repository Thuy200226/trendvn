## How a phase is done

A measurable goal first. Then the change, with a test that fails when the change is removed. Then three reviews: static (read it again, lint, documents match the code), dynamic (run it on real data with before and after numbers), independent (a read-only reviewer tries to break it). Findings are fixed and recorded in the history; nothing is called done without saying what was and was not verified.
